OUR PLATFORM VISION

What is CampusMind AI?

An intelligent, unified academic cockpit and grounded RAG assistant engineered for modern college students and faculty.

Student Centric

CampusMind is designed around real student workflows: instant syllabus clarification, teacher allocations, exam schedule tracking, and interactive subject mentoring.

Grounded AI (RAG)

Utilizes Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) over official college PDFs, SPPU NEP syllabi, and academic handbooks to deliver 100% truthful, hallucination-free answers.

Unified Academic Cockpit

Instead of scattered circulars, students access their enrolled subjects, teacher details, upcoming mid-term dates, and college notices from a single interface.

HOW IT WORKS

From Query to Grounded Answers.

CampusMind combines vector similarity search and large language models for smart learning.

01

Ask Question

Student asks about syllabus, exam formats, lab schedules, or campus policies in English or Hinglish.

02

RAG Retrieval

System performs vector semantic search across indexed VPCSC syllabus PDFs and regulations.

03

Direct Explanation

Delivers concise, point-to-point explanations, study tips, and accurate teacher references.
